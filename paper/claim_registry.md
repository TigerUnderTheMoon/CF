## Upgrade Rules

- `F_PRM_TRAINING` can move to `supported` only with a passing downstream PRM training validation artifact comparing SC-FMA-supervised PRM vs binary-supervised PRM vs raw CIU-supervised PRM.
- `F_REAL_TASK_SC_FMA` can move to `supported` only with fresh, non-overlapping real-task traces passing preregistered smoke gates and full validation gates.
- `F_HUMAN_AUDIT_USEFULNESS` can move to `supported` only after a prospectively specified study documents real evaluator identities or verifiable recruitment provenance, independent assignments, task materials, exclusion rules, adjudication, inter-rater agreement, and uncertainty for all reported human outcomes. The automated rating sheets audited on 2026-07-10 are explicitly excluded; see `paper/information_sciences_submission/human_evaluation_provenance_audit_2026-07-10.md`.
- `M_PRM_BASELINE_CONTEXT` must remain context-only while PRM800K overlap risk is known or unresolved. It cannot be upgraded to external PRM generalization without a clean non-overlap audit and an independently preregistered external benchmark.
- `M_WINDOWED_CALIBRATION` must remain a post hoc locked-split diagnostic unless the window policy is selected on development data and evaluated once on an untouched independent split.
- All diagnostic claims (`C_*`) are retained as motivation for the SC-FMA methodology but are not themselves the primary contribution.
- The methodological claims (`M_*`) constitute the current paper's contribution.
- If PRM800K stratified analysis is blocked, then every active empirical claim used in the KBS package must be downgraded to `stratum_dependent` or `failed_validation`, and the final package must not be labeled submission-ready.
- `M_KBS_AUDIT_DEMONSTRATION` can move from `supported` to `stratum_dependent` or stronger only with a passing external KBS benchmark audit. It cannot be upgraded to production deployment validation.
- `M_KG_ONTOLOGY_EDGE_PILOT` must remain diagnostic/pilot evidence unless a domain KBS workflow supplies typed edges, adjudicated curation targets, and downstream maintenance or error-reduction measurements.
- `M_MUSIQUE_CONSTRUCTED_FEASIBILITY` is downgraded to `archived` (feasibility demonstration only) because the MuSiQue audit labels and the `w_struct` scoring features are both deterministic functions of `step_type` (`src/fma/eval/knowledge_audit.py`: labels assigned per step type in `build_musique_trace`; `raw_local_utility`/`evidence_role`/`bridge_role` features likewise derived from `step_type`). A hand-coded step-type priority rule attains NDCG@25\% = 1.0000 across all 1,415 locked traces, matching the `w_struct`/Ridge/QP rows, so the route provides no independent audit-prioritization evidence. It can be reconsidered as positive evidence only if the label construction is rebuilt from signals orthogonal to `step_type` (e.g., retrieval difficulty, answer-consistency, supporting-fact structure) and a hand-coded step-type baseline is reported below ceiling.
