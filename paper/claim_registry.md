# Claim Registry

Purpose: this file is the manuscript claim contract. Proposal language is not evidence. A claim can be upgraded only when the listed artifacts exist and the listed gates pass.

Status labels:

- `supported`: current artifacts support the bounded claim.
- `stratum_dependent`: current artifacts support the claim only with explicit heterogeneity limits.
- `pilot_blocked`: pilot infrastructure exists, but readiness gates block evidence use.
- `planned`: preregistered route; no validation result yet.
- `failed_validation`: validation artifact exists, but preregistered gates failed.
- `future_validation`: planned work; not a current result.

## Upgrade Rules

- `C_REAL_TASK_PILOT` can move out of `pilot_blocked` only when `outputs/real_task_pilot/readiness_audit.json` reports `pilot_pass: true`.
- `C_S_FMA_V2_FRESH_HOLDOUT` can move out of `planned` only when fresh, non-overlapping GSM8K/HotpotQA holdout traces, scores, replay, Delta-U, and rank-signal artifacts exist under `outputs/s_fma_v2_fresh_holdout/` and satisfy the preregistered gates in `configs/s_fma_v2_fresh_holdout.yaml`; the current manifest/audit is clean but is not a validation result. A drift-disclosed preflight is not `API_PREFLIGHT_READY`, and the failed 20-row stochastic smoke diagnostics are not validation evidence. The approved bounded rerun reports `STOCHASTIC_SMOKE_FAIL_SPARSE_SIGNAL`, `nonzero_delta_rows: 0`, and `next_allowed_step: STOP_OR_REVISE_EVIDENCE_TARGET`, so current status remains `PILOT_BLOCKED`. Deterministic replay wording requires `DETERMINISTIC_REPLAY_ROUTE` drift pass; any further stochastic API work requires explicit bounded approval.
- `C_S_FMA_V2_1_EVIDENCE_TARGET` can move out of `failed_validation` only through a separately preregistered new route, such as v2.2, whose artifacts satisfy their own gates. The current v2.1 full stochastic artifacts must remain failed provenance and must not be rescued by post-hoc threshold or weight tuning. Engineering retry may address timeout/connection completeness, but it cannot directly solve the GSM8K sparse-signal gate or convert the current failed artifact into a pass.
- `C_S_FMA_V2_2_PREREGISTRATION` can move out of `planned` only after a fresh non-overlapping holdout or new preregistered split is locked, v2.2 artifacts are generated under their own output root, and the task-specific/global gates in `configs/s_fma_v2_2_fresh_holdout.yaml` are satisfied. v2.1 full-validation artifacts cannot be used for v2.2 threshold tuning, row selection, or weight fitting.
- `TASK_SPECIFIC_S_FMA_V2_PASS` permits only task-specific or heterogeneous wording for the passing task; it does not permit cross-task expansion.
- `GLOBAL_S_FMA_V2_PASS` requires both GSM8K and HotpotQA to satisfy their task-specific gates and the preregistered rank-signal standard before any cross-task expansion design.
- `C_TRAJECTORY_CONTROLS` can move out of `pilot_blocked` only when every configured control has measured accuracy, token, validity, reflection-count, and cost metrics.
- `C_PRM_FILTERING` can move out of `future_validation` only after `GLOBAL_S_FMA_V2_PASS` or `DOWNSTREAM_PRM_FILTERING_VALIDATION_PASS`, and only when a PRM/filtering experiment exists and compares against vanilla PRM, length-calibrated PRM, token-attribution baselines, and frozen reflection-weight baselines.
- No paper section should upgrade a claim beyond this registry.
